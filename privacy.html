Privacy Policy for Watched bot

Last Updated: September 2026

We value your privacy. This policy explains how our Discord bot handles user data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Discord Identifiers: We store Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs to connect your personal profile to your tracked movies.
  • Movie Lists: We store the movie titles, ratings, and logs you voluntarily submit using bot commands.

2. How We Use Data

Collected data is used strictly to maintain your custom movie logs, show statistics, and display watch lists inside your Discord servers. We do not sell or share your data with any third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is stored securely in our database as long as the bot remains in your server. If you want your movie logging history completely wiped from our systems, please contact the developer via Discord.
